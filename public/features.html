Batch Trades

The Batch Trades command is designed for maximum efficiency, allowing you to request up to 6 Pokémon in a single session. This is perfect for completing competitive teams or moving multiple sets quickly.

1. Showdown Text Integration

Copy your Pokémon sets from Showdown and separate each individual Pokémon using three dashes (---).

$batchtrade Miraidon @ Choice Specs Ability: Hadron Engine ... --- Flutter Mane @ Booster Energy ...

2. Archive Transmission (.ZIP)

If you have your Pokémon saved as .pk9 files, bundle them into a single ZIP archive and upload it.

$bt (Upload .zip attachment)

Protocol Constraints

  • Session Limit: Maximum of 6 Pokémon per batch transmission.
  • Preparation: Ensure you have enough "fodder" Pokémon ready in your game boxes.
  • Legality Check: Every Pokémon must pass the server-side legality check for the batch to process.

Pokémon Cloning

The $clone command allows you to create an identical duplicate of any Pokémon you already own in Scarlet or Violet. This uses our proprietary "Safe-Scan" method—you never actually have to trade away your original Pokémon!

Synthesis Workflow (Show & Copy)

  1. 01. Type $clone in the designated Discord channel to wake the node.
  2. 02. The bot will DM you a Link Code. Enter this in the game's Poke Portal.
  3. 03. Select the Pokémon you want to duplicate and choose "Show" (do not offer).
  4. 04. Once the scan completes, the bot will immediately offer the clone in the trade window.
  5. 05. Exchange a junk Pokémon for the clone. Your original remains in your possession.
